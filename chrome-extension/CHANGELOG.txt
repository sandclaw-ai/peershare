SandClaw.ai PeerShare Proxy (Chrome extension) — changes
=========================================================

0.1.0 — 2026-11-13

  First release: the PeerShare proxy's job, done by a browser extension
  instead of a script.
  - Relays the PeerShare page's requests to services on this computer and
    private networks (localhost, 10/8, 172.16/12, 192.168/16, 100.64/10,
    IPv6 unique-local); refuses everything else, and .local names.
  - Answers only https://peershare.sandclaw.ai and pages on localhost and
    127.0.0.1.
  - HTTP only: no WebSockets, no per-guest cookie jars; redirects are
    followed.
