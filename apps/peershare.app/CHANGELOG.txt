PeerShare (peershare.sandclaw.ai) — changes
===========================================

The site: the Sharer's page (the hub), the Guest's invite page, and the help.
The proxy script and the browser extension have their own changelogs:
proxy/CHANGELOG.txt and chrome-extension/CHANGELOG.txt.

2026-11-13 — One hub across tabs; diagnostics

  Fixed
  - PeerShare open in several tabs ran one hub per tab, all with the same
    identity. A Guest could reach a tab that had not made their invite and
    was turned away as if the Sharer's computer were not answering. Now one
    tab runs the hub and every other tab shows it and acts on it: the same
    services, sharing state, guests and invites everywhere. When that tab
    closes, another takes over and shares again.
  - Proxy settings saved in one tab are used by the tab that runs the hub.

  Added
  - Guests are told what actually went wrong: "Dirk's PeerShare doesn't
    know this invite", "Dirk's computer did not let you in" with the hub's
    own words, and "isn't answering" only when nothing answered. Every
    failure shows "What happened".
  - A diagnostics log on every page, always on and kept across reloads:
    join steps, the hub's answers, invites made and redeemed or refused,
    guests coming and going, probe results, every error with its causes.
    "Copy diagnostics" on the hub page and on a Guest's failed join;
    peershareDiagnostics() in the browser console.

2026-11-13 — Browser extension; local network advice

  Added
  - The PeerShare browser extension as an alternative to the proxy script:
    the page finds it and uses it, and the proxy card says "Browser
    extension connected". Download and install steps on the proxy card and
    in the help (/extension/peershare-proxy-extension.zip).
  - Services blocked by the browser's local network access say so, with
    the steps to allow it (every permission Chrome has for it is read).

  Changed
  - Safety tips use an "i" pill instead of "?".
