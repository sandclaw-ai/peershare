PeerShare Proxy — changes
=========================

0.1.1 — 2026-11-13

  Fixed
  - Services that compress their responses (gzip, br, deflate) were relayed
    with their Content-Encoding header but an already decoded body. Browsers
    then failed to read every such page ("Failed to fetch"), and the
    PeerShare hub showed the service as "checking" forever.

  Changed
  - Start-up output: the version, labels in one column, one allowed page
    per line, and the key (only when --key is given).

0.1.0 — 2026-11-12

  First versioned release: the Bridge on 127.0.0.1 (default port 9090),
  launched with
    curl -fsSL https://peershare.sandclaw.ai/proxy/peershare-proxy.sh | sh
  Options: --port, --key, --origin (repeatable), --no-cookie-jars.
  Runs under Node, Bun or Deno.
